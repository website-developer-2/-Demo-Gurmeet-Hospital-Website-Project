01 — ABOUT THE HOSPITAL

Multispeciality care,
rooted in the neighbourhood.

Located near Jourian Bhattian, Gher Sodhian in Gur Mandi, Gurmeet Multispeciality Hospital is built around a simple idea — quality treatment shouldn’t be far from home. From walk-in consultations to emergency admissions, our team keeps the hospital running around the clock, backed by an in-house pharmacy, diagnostic services and a dedicated ambulance on standby.
